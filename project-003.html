[ 01 ] OVERVIEW

The Problem

Degree planning is manual, and getting prerequisites wrong costs a quarter — but say it in your own words, and say who you watched struggle with it.

What I Built

A drag-and-drop degree planning tool: students arrange courses across terms directly, and the interface holds the constraints so they don't have to.

Behind it runs an auto-planning algorithm that sequences courses by prerequisite chain and difficulty, generating a viable plan rather than leaving the student to solve the ordering by hand.

Outcome

Reduced scheduling time by 80% in testing with 30+ students.
