01

Colour

Four values and one accent. Change --accent and the whole site rebrands: every hover, active state and highlight reads from it. Never use #FFF or #000. A true white kills the grain; a true black gives photographs a hard edge.

--paper#F7F6F5
--ink#0D0D0C
--accent#1F3FE0
--ruleink 10%
--hairink 13%
--muteink 42%
--faintink 52%

04

Motion

One curve for movement, cubic-bezier(.19,1,.22,1). Plain ease for colour. Nothing bounces, nothing spins, nothing exceeds 0.7s. The entire vocabulary is one move: something slides a few pixels toward the reader.

the nudge scale

4px a number · 5px a list item · 6px a title · 8px a link that leaves the page. Past 8px it stops being a hint and becomes an animation.

the opt-out

Every effect sits on top of a page that already works. prefers-reduced-motion removes all of it and nothing breaks.
