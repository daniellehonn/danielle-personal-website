PDF Parser Test Suite

The Problem

Every PDF parser claims to be the most accurate, and none of the claims are checkable. Choosing between LlamaParse, ContextForce and Jina.ai meant trusting marketing copy or eyeballing a handful of documents — neither of which tells you how a parser behaves on the cases that actually break: scanned pages, dense tables, mathematical formulas.

What I Built

A test suite that benchmarks each parser across the four areas where they diverge: basic text extraction, OCR extraction, table extraction and formula detection. Defining those evaluation criteria was most of the work — deciding what "correct" means for a parsed table is harder than measuring it.

Testing is automated end-to-end with Pytest, and scoring is handed to an LLM rather than a human, so every parser is judged against the same rubric without the bias that creeps in when you already have a favourite.

Outcome

Produced a corpus of deliberately complex PDFs that differentiates parsers precisely — published so other people can run their own evaluations against the same test data instead of starting from scratch.
