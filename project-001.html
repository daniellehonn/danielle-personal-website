The Problem

Only you can write this: what was broken about using AI while browsing, who felt it, and why it was worth building. Two or three sentences. Replace this block.

What I Built

A Chrome extension that embeds AI directly into everyday browsing — pulling insights from YouTube, recognising code on GitHub, and extracting content from arbitrary webpages so it can be handed to a model without copy-paste.

On top of that sits a configurable prompt management system: users save, customise and tune their own prompts, so the quality of the LLM output is something they control rather than something the extension guesses at.
