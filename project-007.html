[ 01 ] OVERVIEW

The Problem

Climate advice is generic where the problem is local. Air quality data exists in public feeds and carbon guidance exists in listicles, but neither is grounded in where you actually are right now — so the advice arrives untethered from the conditions it's meant to respond to, and gets ignored.

What I Built

You enter a city. Behind that single input, an orchestrator dispatches a pipeline of three autonomous agents, each with one job:

Emissions Scout fetches live AQI from EPA AirNow, PM2.5 from Open-Meteo and CO₂ estimates — in parallel, so the slowest feed sets the pace rather than the sum of them. Action Router passes that real-time picture to ASI1 Mini and gets back three personalised, actionable recommendations grounded in the actual numbers. Media Composer renders the result as an infographic card through Cloudinary URL transformations, so the output is shareable rather than trapped in the app.

A FastAPI orchestrator coordinates the three over Fetch.ai's uAgents framework, and the front end is a React dashboard with a chat interface, live metrics panels and an action feed.
