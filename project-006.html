[ 01 ] OVERVIEW

The Problem

Degree planning is manual, and getting prerequisites wrong costs a quarter — but say it in your own words, and say who you watched struggle with it.

What I Built

A drag-and-drop degree planning tool: students arrange courses across terms directly, and the interface holds the constraints so they don't have to. Colour coding shows what's ready to take and dependency lines draw the prerequisite relationships, so an invalid plan looks wrong before it is wrong.

Behind it runs an auto-planning algorithm that sequences courses by prerequisite chain and difficulty, balancing workload across terms and generating a viable plan rather than leaving the student to solve the ordering by hand. Courses import from a spreadsheet template and plans export for reuse.

Outcome

Reduced scheduling time by 80% in testing with 30+ students.
